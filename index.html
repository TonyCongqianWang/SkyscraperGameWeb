How to play

Fill every row and column with heights 1 through N, without duplicates. Edge clues tell you how many skyscrapers are visible from that side. Taller buildings hide smaller ones. Click an edge clue to check that view. Double-click a cell to open a nearby number pad. More detailed illustrated rules ↗
